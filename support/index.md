# RollBase support

Questions, problems or ideas: email hello@rollbase.ai.

## Common questions

**How do I log a session?** Tap + on the Dashboard or pick a day in the Tracker. Add your drilling, positional sparring and rolling time, the submissions you finished or got caught in, and the positions you won or lost.

**Where do A-Game and To Improve come from?** From the sessions you log. A-Game shows what you finish and win most, To Improve shows where you get tapped or lose position. Switch between week, month and year at the top.

**Why won't a video play?** Technique videos stream, so they need an internet connection. Everything else in the app works offline.

**I used RollBase with an account before 2.0. Where is my history?** The first time you open 2.0 while still signed in, the app copies your sessions to your phone automatically. If something is missing, email us.

**How do I change my training days or reminders?** Open the menu on the Dashboard, then Profile.

**How do I delete my data?** Profile, then Erase All Data, removes everything stored on your phone. For data from an account in an older version, email us.

**Is there a subscription?** No. RollBase 2.0 is a one-time purchase. If you still have a subscription from an older version, cancel it in your Apple ID settings under Subscriptions.
